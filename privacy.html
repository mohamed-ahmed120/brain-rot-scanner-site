Privacy Policy

Brain Rot Scanner

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Notice: This is a preliminary privacy policy. The app's actual data handling must be reviewed before public release.

1. Information and Permissions

Brain Rot Scanner may request Android permissions needed for its features, such as screen capture initiated by the user and usage access to identify the foreground application. The permissions requested and their use should be reviewed in the app before release.

2. Screen Capture

Screen capture is initiated through Android's permission system. Users should stop a capture session when they no longer want the app to capture screen content. Do not display private information while a capture session is active.

3. Images and Character References

The app supports character reference images. How these images are stored, processed, backed up, or shared depends on the app's actual implementation and must be verified before release.

4. Data Sharing and Advertising

Advertising or analytics services may be added in the future. If they are added, this policy must be updated to identify the services used, the information they process, and the available privacy choices.

5. Data Security and Retention

Data storage, retention, and deletion practices must be confirmed against the released version of the app. Users should avoid storing sensitive information in the app.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is not intended to collect personal information from children. The developer must review applicable age and privacy requirements before distributing the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the app's features and data practices change. The date above will be revised when changes are published.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Brain Rot Scanner, you can contact us at: brainrotscanner.app@gmail.com
